Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how Streamium collects, uses, and safeguards your personal information.

📅 Last Updated: August 2, 2026

1 1. Introduction

  • Welcome to Streamium. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.
  • This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect your information when you access or use our website and services.
  • By using Streamium, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2 2. Information We Collect

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your login method (Google Sign-In or Email OTP).
  • Your profile picture, when provided through Google Sign-In.
  • Your IP address, browser type, operating system, and device type.
  • Your approximate location based on your IP address.
  • Your login history.
  • Payment transaction details and subscription information.
  • Other information necessary to provide and secure our services.

3 3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To verify your identity.
  • To process payments and activate subscriptions.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To improve our services.
  • To maintain platform security and prevent fraud.
  • To communicate important service updates.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

4 4. How We Protect Your Data

  • We implement reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.
  • While we strive to safeguard your information, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5 5. Sharing Your Information

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We may share limited information with trusted service providers only when necessary to process payments.
  • We may share limited information when necessary to authenticate users or provide technical services.
  • We may share limited information when necessary to comply with legal obligations.
  • We may share limited information when necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of Streamium and its users.

6 6. Children’s Privacy

  • Streamium is not intended for children under the age of 18 without the consent of a parent or legal guardian.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.
  • If we become aware that such information has been collected unintentionally, we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

7 7. Payment Information

  • Payments are processed securely through our authorized third-party payment providers.
  • Streamium does not store your complete debit card, credit card, UPI PIN, bank account password, or other sensitive payment credentials.
  • We may retain limited payment information such as transaction ID, payment status, subscription plan, payment amount, and payment date.
  • This limited information is retained for payment verification, subscription activation, customer support, billing records, and fraud prevention.

8 8. Cookies and Tracking

  • Streamium may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain login sessions and remember your preferences.
  • Cookies also help us improve website performance, analyze website usage, and enhance your overall experience.
  • You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.
  • Disabling cookies may affect certain website features.

9 9. Your Rights

  • You may request access to your personal information.
  • You may request corrections to inaccurate information.
  • You may request deletion of your account where applicable.
  • You may contact us regarding the processing of your personal data.
  • We will respond to valid requests within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable laws.

10 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • Streamium may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
  • Any changes will become effective when the updated version is published on this page.
  • Continued use of the Service after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
